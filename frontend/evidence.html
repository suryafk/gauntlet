What this page is

The live demo runs in mock mode: call transcripts are scripted, so the dashboard shows the workflow without spending credits. This page shows the parts that ran for real, outside the demo.

A real simulated call, graded by Gemini

Gemini (gemini-3.5-flash-lite) played both sides in text: the mid-sentence corrector persona, and the PayEasy target agent working from its prompt (the first draft of v1). Nothing was scripted. Then the Gemini judge graded the transcript.

AgentNamaste, PayEasy mein aapka swagat hai. How can I help you today?
CallerHaan namaste, umm, mujhe ek bill payment schedule karna tha.
AgentMain bilkul madad kar sakti hoon. Kripya apna 8-digit account number batayiye.
CallerHaan, mera account number hai 8820 1177.
AgentThank you. Kitna amount aap pay karna chahte hain?
CallerUmm, pachaas— nahi nahi, pachpan hazaar rupees pay karne hain.
AgentThank you. Kripya payment ki date batayiye, jaise ki 25 October.
CallerUmm, date rakhni hai 30 October 2026.
AgentThank you. Your payment of 55000 rupees for account number 88201177 has been scheduled for 30 October.
What the agent sent to its schedule_payment tool
{"account_number": "88201177", "amount": 55000, "payment_date": "30 October", "waive_late_fee": false}

How it was scored

MetricResultReason given
Extraction✓ passChecked in code: account number and amount match what the caller meant (55,000, not the 50,000 they first said).
Interrupt✓ passGemini: the agent adapted to the caller's correction from 50,000 to 55,000 without re-asking.
Policy✕ failGemini: the agent never read the amount back to get a yes before scheduling.
Task✕ failGemini: same reason as Policy.

Two honest notes. First, the policy failure is the real flaw: the agent scheduled the payment without reading anything back. Second, the Task verdict is arguably wrong, because the payment was scheduled. That's exactly why Gauntlet has a Review tab, where a person spot-checks the judge and the agreement rate is tracked.

This draft of v1 also let the agent use the corrected amount despite a "keep the first number" instruction. So v1's flaws were rewritten as firm company rules, and scripts/check_flaws.py now checks that each one fires.

Real Bolna integration, built but not yet run against live calls

  • scripts/setup_bolna.py creates the target and caller agents through Bolna's v2 agent API and attaches the target to its phone number.
  • The target agent's schedule_payment custom tool posts what it extracted to Gauntlet, so the target's side of each call is captured.
  • Each call's webhook is matched to the right run, and the target's side is joined to its caller-side call by time and transcript similarity.
  • Before a run, the target agent's prompt is switched to the run's version through the Bolna API, so a patched re-run dials the patched agent.

Real mode needs one Bolna phone number for the target agent; the callers dial out from Bolna's shared numbers. It's switched on with BOLNA_MODE=real.
